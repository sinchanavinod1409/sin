# Frame portfolio

A dependency-free, responsive one-page portfolio for a freelance video editor and social content creator. Run locally or in preview with `npm start` (listens on port 3000 by default).

## Before sharing publicly

Update the clearly marked placeholder details and links in `public/index.html`:

- Replace `[Your Name]` and `@yourhandle` with your name and Instagram username/profile URL.
- Replace `hello@yourname.com` in the email links and displayed contact details with your email address.
- Replace the placeholder phone number in the WhatsApp URL with your country code and number (digits only).
- Replace each project card's concept copy and `data-url` with the matching real work/case-study link. Replace the generated monochrome concept frames with your own samples when ready.
- Adjust the service and introduction copy to match your voice and offer.

The four gallery thumbnails use original generated concept imagery and are deliberately labeled as replaceable preview material. All personal names, social profiles, project URLs, email addresses, and phone numbers are placeholders; verify those links before launch.
